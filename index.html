How Adoption Works

Clear, thoughtful steps help dogs and adopters start off on the right paw.

1

Send an inquiry

Tell us which dog interests you and a little about your household and experience.

2

Have a conversation

We'll discuss the dog's known needs, temperament, care, and whether the match feels right.

3

Plan the next step

If it's a good fit, we'll explain the meet-and-greet, paperwork, and any adoption fee before you commit.

Adoption fees, eligibility, veterinary records, and transport arrangements should be confirmed directly before any payment or commitment.
